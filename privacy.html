Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026.

This tool is used only by the owner of the More Than the Box Score accounts to send our own stats videos to our own TikTok account as drafts.

What we access: with your permission, basic TikTok profile information (open ID, display name) and permission to upload videos to your TikTok inbox as drafts (scopes user.info.basic and video.upload).

What we store: an access token and refresh token, kept encrypted and used only to upload drafts. We do not collect, sell or share any personal data, and we do not access other users' data.

Removing access: you can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services.

Contact: via Instagram @more_than_the_box.
